Our story

Bold shapes,
quiet luxury.

Taarougem was born from a love of bold, sculptural jewellery: pieces with weight, shine and presence. Every bracelet is designed and threaded by hand, one stone at a time.

We are drawn to what the earth makes slowly: the landscapes of jasper, the light of tiger eye, the depth of ebony. Wear one alone as a statement, or stack them high.
